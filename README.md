- AI accelerates. Humans decide.
- Discovery comes before commitment.
- Product Intent locks before Engineering planning.
- Product drives Engineering.
- Uncertainty, dependencies, risk, and tradeoffs should remain visible.
- Every approval should state what it permits and what it does not permit.
- Good Product systems are usable, auditable, and trusted by the people who depend on them.
